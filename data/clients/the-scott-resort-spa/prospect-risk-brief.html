Across 2,941 captured public guest reviews of this property, 12 mention entertainment in any form, positive or negative. That is 0.4% of the guest record discussing a program the property runs on a published weekly schedule.

Of those, 210 were written in the most recent twelve months, and 1 of those mentions entertainment.

A mention rate measures visibility in guest voice, not guest satisfaction. Whether 0.4% sits above or below comparable resorts in this market is a benchmark question this brief does not answer.

Signals worth a closer look

  • 3 of 5 advertised programs are never named by a single captured guest.
  • 1 guest asked for entertainment the property already runs on its published schedule, which indicates the program did not reach that guest during the stay.
  • 3 of the verified entertainment mentions are negative, and 7 are positive. Both appear verbatim below.
  • Entertainment occupies 0.4% of the captured guest record. Whether that is normal for this class of resort is a benchmark comparison, not something this brief asserts.

Questions this evidence leaves open

Each question below comes from a gap the public record cannot close. They are the decisions a full audit is built to resolve.

  1. In the most recent twelve months the property drew 210 public reviews, of which 1 mentions entertainment. Is current programming landing with today's guest, and is that number moving up or down against prior years?
  2. The property publicly advertises 5 recurring entertainment programs, and 12 of 2,941 captured guest reviews mention entertainment at all. Is that because the programming underperforms, or because it is not reaching the guests who write reviews? Those are different problems with different costs.
  3. 3 of the 5 advertised programs are never named by any captured guest. Are those nights drawing guests who simply do not write about them, or are they running to rooms that are effectively empty?
  4. 1 guest asked for entertainment the property already runs on a published schedule. How many others left without ever learning it existed, and what does that cost in repeat stays and on-property spend?
  5. Among guests who did discuss entertainment, 7 spoke positively and 3 negatively. Is that split concentrated in particular nights, outlets, or seasons, and which side is growing?
  6. How does this property's entertainment visibility compare with directly competing resorts in the same market, and which competitor is capturing the guest attention this property is not?
