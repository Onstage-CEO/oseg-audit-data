tripadvisor · 2019-11-08 · 5/5view source

Once you unpack at the Phoenician Hotel, it's hard to break away As you should expect of a luxury resort, the Phoenician Hotel in Scottsdale, Arizona, has a lot to offer. So once you've unpacked, you find it difficult to break away. Like breakfast at Mowry and Cotton, lunch at the Phoenician Tavern and dinner at J & G Steakhouse. The new tavern, which opened in 2018, offers opportunities to enjoy some pub grab after a round of golf or sample some local craft beers while sitting in a booth and watching the game of the day on TV or snacking on this and that while sitting on the indoor/outdoor bar patio with views of the lush, green golf course. Open at 11 a.m. daily, the tavern advertises the longest Happy Hour in town. My wife and I stopped by for a quick lunch before another round of sight-seeing. We snacked on a dozen chicken wings with BBQ sauce, a bowl of tomato bisque, a plate of fish and chips with ale battered cod, tartar sauce and fries, a Patty Melt sandwich with prime beef, Swiss cheese, 1,000 Island dressing and braised onions on griddled marble rye bread and two large slices of hot apple pie with vanilla ice cream. Pub grub? Whatever you choose to label it, it really hits the spot.
