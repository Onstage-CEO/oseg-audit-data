The defining entertainment finding at The Phoenician Resort and Spa is that your most talked-about live programming is happening at the bar and lounge level, not on a main stage. The Phoenician Tavern's Saturday dueling pianos and the weekend live music at Thirsty Camel are the two formats guests name by venue, and both sit at the heart of how guests describe evenings here.

Entertainment is one meaningful theme among several at this property, appearing in roughly one in eight reviews. It sits well behind service and staff, food and beverage, and the pool and amenities, which lead your review set by a wide margin. That ranking matters: guests come for service and the resort grounds first, and the live program supports those evenings rather than carrying them.

Within that role, in-venue entertainment rates about on par with the property's overall standard. The signal is strong but not uniform, and the value of this report is in finding the specific venues and formats that pull above or below your own line, rather than offering a single verdict.

The satisfaction landscape

Across roughly 6, 000 verified reviews, service and staff is the dominant theme by a clear margin, followed closely by food and beverage and the pool and amenities. Cleanliness and the room, value, and check-in and wait fill out the middle of the picture. Entertainment and atmosphere sits last of the named themes at around 13 percent of reviews.

For these specific guests, that order is the context for everything the live program does. People are choosing The Phoenician for the staff, the dining, and the resort grounds, and the evening entertainment is the texture layered over those visits. When a guest writes about dueling pianos or a Saturday band, they are almost always describing it as part of a dinner or a drinks evening that was already about the food and the service. That is why your venue-level entertainment matters more here than a standalone show would: it is woven into the themes guests already rate highest.

Entertainment deep-dive

The headline entertainment story is The Phoenician Tavern. Its Saturday dueling pianos are the format guests single out most clearly, and the venue carries a solid rating across more than 250 reviews. Guests pair the pianos with the casual menu, the boozy milkshakes, and what one called the best wine list on the property, which tells you the entertainment is landing as part of a well-rounded evening rather than as a novelty. The Tavern's rating sits a touch below the resort's overall standard, which is worth watching, but the dueling pianos themselves are a genuine draw and the clearest signature live format you have.

Thirsty Camel is the second pillar. Its weekend-evening live music draws steady praise for a mellow, romantic ambiance, with guests describing staying through to the end of a set over drinks. The pattern across both venues is consistent: live music inside, a relaxed atmosphere, and an audience that treats the performance as the reason to linger. One guest's account of a soft-rock band at Thirsty Camel with people up dancing captures the upside when the format and the room match.

So the program rates about on par with the property as a whole, with the bars and lounges doing the heavy lifting. Restaurants are the second-most-mentioned setting for entertainment and atmosphere, while the pool decks and common areas register only lightly in this context. The watch-item is specific: the Tavern's overall rating trails the resort standard slightly even as its dueling pianos are a strength, so the opportunity is to lift the rest of that venue's experience to the level its signature night already reaches, rather than to change the entertainment.

The opportunity

The most concrete move is to protect and extend what already works at The Phoenician Tavern and Thirsty Camel. The Tavern's Saturday dueling pianos and the Thirsty Camel weekend live music are the two formats guests name, and they are the natural foundation for a more consistent week-to-week live calendar in the bars and lounges, where your entertainment mentions concentrate.

Because the Tavern's overall rating sits slightly under the resort standard while its dueling pianos are a clear strength, the goal is consistency: bring the quality of a signature Saturday to more evenings, so a guest's experience does not depend on which night they happen to visit. Restaurants are the second setting where entertainment and atmosphere come up, which suggests room to extend a light, well-matched live touch into dining evenings in the same mellow register guests already praise. The open-web signal points the same way, with editorial coverage noting a live piano performance during the afternoon tea seating, an example of music supporting an experience rather than dominating it. Onstage's role here is to help even out the live calendar across venues and nights so the strength you already have shows up reliably, working as one team with your operation.

A note on sound. The most frequent sound-related complaint in these reviews concerns piped background music in the pool and common areas, not the live performances. That is a house-audio setting, addressed separately from the live entertainment assessed in this report; the live program is not the source.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 6,010 captured public guest reviews. Platform-level public totals were not refreshed for every source in the latest capture status, so this report does not present a summed public-review universe. The reviews analyzed span Oct 2002 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded. Where a source's captured count is below its public total, the difference is typically rating-only entries (a star score with no written text), which carry no analyzable content and are not part of the figures.

This report draws on roughly 6, 000 verified public guest reviews of The Phoenician Resort and Spa across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google, the large majority in English. We isolated the reviews that touch entertainment and atmosphere, read them at the venue level, and weighed them against the property's overall rating and theme mix so that the live program is judged against your own standard rather than an outside benchmark. Venue-level findings reflect named venues only, and the open-web references are limited to verified published editorial.
