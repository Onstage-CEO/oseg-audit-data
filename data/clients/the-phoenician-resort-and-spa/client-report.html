The defining entertainment story at The Phoenician is happening at two named venues that pull in opposite directions on the property's evening atmosphere. The Phoenician Tavern hosts dueling pianos on Saturday nights, and Thirsty Camel runs live music from Thursday through Saturday. Both are guest favorites and both sit a little below the resort's overall rating, which tells you the entertainment itself lands well but the venues carry the usual food, service and wait-time variance that affects every dining room here.

Entertainment is one meaningful theme among several at this property, present in roughly one in eight reviews. It rates about on par with the resort's overall standard, so this is not a turnaround situation. The useful work is finding which formats and venues pull above the line and which pull below it, then protecting what is already working on the live-music and dueling-piano nights.

The single watch-item worth a GM's attention is not the programmed entertainment at all. It is ambient sound. One guest described being unable to find a quiet seat anywhere near the three pools because of music through the speakers, including in the bathrooms and fitness center. That is a different lever from the live programming, and it is worth separating the two.

The satisfaction landscape

For these guests, the conversation is led by service and staff, which appears in nearly two-thirds of reviews, followed closely by food and beverage and by the pool and amenities, each in roughly half. Cleanliness and room, value, and check-in wait times fill out the middle of the mix. Entertainment and atmosphere sits at the smaller end of the theme set, present in about one in eight reviews.

That placement matters for how you read the entertainment signal. Because food and beverage and the pool are the dominant themes, most entertainment mentions arrive attached to a meal or a poolside afternoon rather than as a standalone night out. The dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern and the live music at Thirsty Camel are praised inside reviews that are also rating the food, the wine list and the service. When you read this property's entertainment, you are largely reading what happens inside its bars, lounges and restaurants.

Entertainment deep-dive

The headline entertainment here is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Saturday nights. Guests single it out as fun, and the same reviews praise the casual menu, the boozy milkshakes the venue is known for, and what one guest called the best wine list of any bar or restaurant on the property. The Tavern rates a touch below the resort's overall standard, which reflects the full dining experience rather than the entertainment, since the piano nights themselves draw consistent praise.

The second pillar is live music at Thirsty Camel, Thursday through Saturday. Guests describe a mellow, romantic atmosphere, an inside room with live music and a quieter candlelit patio outside, and staying past last song because they were enjoying it. Thirsty Camel also rates slightly below the overall line, again on service pace and busy-night capacity rather than on the music.

Across the venue mix, bars and lounges carry the largest share of entertainment mentions, with restaurants second and the pool decks and common areas a distant third. The in-venue entertainment rating lands almost exactly on the property's overall rating, which confirms the on-par read. What is working is clear: the live-music nights at Thirsty Camel and the dueling pianos at the Tavern, both tied to strong food and beverage and attentive staff. The specific watch-item is separate from any of this programming, and it concerns ambient sound around the pools and common areas, where one guest could not find a quiet seat. The two should be managed independently, because the live programming is an asset and the piped poolside audio is a comfort question.

The guest voice

The honest read is that most guests who mention entertainment praise it, and a meaningful minority did not. Of the 417 reviews that touch entertainment, 34 rated the experience one or two stars, against 249 at five stars. The praise clusters tightly around the live music at Thirsty Camel and the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern, with guests describing dancing, lingering past the last song and standout evenings. The critical voice is narrower and points mainly at ambient music volume around the pools, restaurants and common areas rather than at the programmed acts themselves.

The opportunity

The clearest opening is to protect and extend what already performs. The dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern and the live music at Thirsty Camel are the two formats guests name by venue, so consistency on those nights, the right act fit for the mellow Thirsty Camel room, and steady service pace on busy weekends will hold the experience that is already drawing five-star reviews. Onstage can act as one partner to keep those Thursday-through-Saturday and Saturday slots reliably booked and matched to each room's character.

There is also a quieter atmosphere question to address separately. The pool decks and common areas barely register in entertainment mentions, and the one pointed complaint there is about piped sound being inescapable rather than about live programming. A reviewed approach to ambient zones, where some pool and dining seating stays genuinely low-volume, would remove the property's sharpest entertainment-adjacent criticism without touching the live nights that guests love. Both moves are grounded in this resort's own reviews, not a generic program.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 6,010 captured public guest reviews. Platform-level public totals were not refreshed for every source in the latest capture status, so this report does not present a summed public-review universe. The reviews analyzed span Oct 2002 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded. Where a source's captured count is below its public total, the difference is typically rating-only entries (a star score with no written text), which carry no analyzable content and are not part of the figures.

This audit draws on 6, 010 verified public guest reviews of The Phoenician Resort and Spa captured across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia and Google, the large majority in English. Theme shares, venue mentions and star distributions are read directly from that review set, and venue-level findings reference only venues guests named, principally The Phoenician Tavern and Thirsty Camel. Open-web scanning of forums, social platforms and travel editorial was used for context only; no rating, count or claim in this report rests on anything outside the verified reviews.
