The Phoenician Resort and Spa earns a strong overall rating built across more than four thousand verified guest reviews on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google. The picture that emerges is of a property guests genuinely admire, led by service and food and beverage, with a sense of place that follows them from the restaurants to the bars and lounges to the pool decks.

Entertainment and atmosphere are part of that picture. Guests who mention it score it close to the property average, and they tend to talk about it in the same breath as ambiance, food, and service. That is a healthy sign. Atmosphere is not a separate event here; it is woven into the experience guests already value.

This report reads the themes guests raise on their own, looks closely at where atmosphere lives across your venues, and points to where a focused entertainment program can lift an already good experience. The posture throughout is simple: this is a strength to protect and build on, not a problem to solve.

The satisfaction landscape

When guests choose what to write about, service and staff lead by a wide margin, followed by food and beverage, then pool and amenities. These are the pillars of the Phoenician experience, and they set the context for everything else. Value, cleanliness, and check-in appear at more modest levels, the normal texture of a large and busy resort.

Entertainment and atmosphere sit further down the list by volume, which is typical and expected. What matters is not how often it comes up but how it comes up. Guests rarely treat atmosphere as a standalone topic. They fold it into praise for the wider stay, the feel of a room, the energy of a bar, the mood by the pool. That tells us atmosphere is doing quiet work in support of the themes guests care about most.

Entertainment deep-dive

Guests who mention entertainment and atmosphere rate the property close to its overall average, which puts atmosphere on solid footing rather than at the margins. The pattern across venues is the most useful part of the read. Atmosphere shows up most in the restaurants, then in the bars and lounges, and then across the pool decks and common areas. In other words, the places guests already love are also the places where mood and energy are noticed.

What is working is the connection between food, service, and feel. When guests describe a strong evening here, ambiance is part of the same sentence as the meal and the staff. That integration is hard to manufacture and easy to take for granted.

The watch-item is consistency of presence. Atmosphere is mentioned less often than the larger themes, which suggests it is felt in some moments and not yet a dependable signature across every venue and every part of the day. The opportunity is to make the good feeling repeatable, so more guests leave describing it without being prompted.

The opportunity

The clearest path is to build on the venues guests already single out. The restaurants and the bars and lounges are where atmosphere is most noticed, so a consistent live music and ambiance program in those rooms would reinforce a strength rather than introduce something new. Programming the pool decks and common areas during the day extends that same feel into the hours when many guests are most relaxed and most present.

The goal is dependability. A planned rhythm of music and presence across the week, matched to the natural energy of each space, helps turn atmosphere from a happy accident into a reliable part of the Phoenician name. Because guests here connect feel so closely to food and service, even modest, well-placed programming tends to lift the whole evening.

Onstage can act as one partner across these spaces, planning the program, booking and managing talent, and keeping the experience consistent so your team can stay focused on the guest.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 4,203 captured public guest reviews. Platform-level public totals were not refreshed for every source in the latest capture status, so this report does not present a summed public-review universe. The reviews analyzed span Oct 2017 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded.

This report is based only on verified public guest reviews, more than four thousand of them, captured across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google. The large majority are in English. We read the themes guests raise on their own and isolated the reviews that touch on entertainment and atmosphere, then looked at how those mentions distribute across the property's venues. No ratings or quotes were modeled or invented; every figure traces back to the underlying reviews.
