The Phoenician Resort and Spa holds a strong overall guest standing across more than four thousand verified reviews, with a high average rating and a relatively small share of low-star feedback. The story guests tell is led, first and foremost, by service and staff, followed by food and beverage and the pool and amenities. These are the pillars of the property's reputation.

Within that picture, entertainment and atmosphere is a clear, if quieter, strength. Where guests do speak to the social life of the resort, the bars, lounges, and dining rooms, they rate the experience above the property's entertainment baseline and consistently positive. This is an asset already working in your favor.

The opportunity is not to repair anything. It is to give a proven strength more presence, so that the atmosphere guests already enjoy in your bars and restaurants becomes a more deliberate part of the Phoenician story.

The satisfaction landscape

The theme mix is telling. Service and staff dominate the conversation, appearing in well over half of all reviews, which signals that guests come to the Phoenician for how they are treated. Food and beverage follows as the second most discussed theme, and the pool and amenities rank third. Together these define what guests value and what they return for.

Entertainment and atmosphere sits further down the list by volume, which is typical and healthy for a resort of this profile. It is not a complaint category. It surfaces most often as part of the dining and lounge experience, which is exactly where atmosphere does its quiet work. When guests talk about the social feel of an evening here, they are talking about the venues where your service and food and beverage strengths already meet.

Entertainment deep-dive

Of the reviews that touch on entertainment and atmosphere, the average score sits above the property's entertainment baseline and reads as a genuine positive. The volume is modest but consistent, drawn from a meaningful set of mentions.

The venue pattern is the most useful signal here. Bars and lounges lead by a wide margin, with restaurants close behind. Together they account for the large majority of atmosphere mentions. The dedicated nightlife and live-music space and the pool decks and common areas appear far less often. The reading is straightforward: the atmosphere guests notice and praise lives inside your bars and dining rooms, where it rides on the back of strong service and food and beverage.

What is working is the lounge and dining atmosphere, a strength worth protecting. The watch-item is reach. Because the social energy is concentrated in a few venues, the pool decks, common areas, and the dedicated live-music space are not yet pulling their weight in the guest conversation. That is headroom, not a problem.

The opportunity

The clearest path is to extend the atmosphere that already works. The bars, lounges, and restaurants are where guests feel the social life of the resort, so that is where a more deliberate live-music and ambiance program will land most naturally, reinforcing a strength rather than introducing something new.

From there, the pool decks, common areas, and the dedicated live-music venue represent quiet space. A light, well-placed presence in these areas, matched to the rhythm of the day, can broaden the moments where guests notice the atmosphere without changing the character of the property. The aim is presence and consistency, delivered through one team so the experience feels seamless across venues.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 4,203 captured public guest reviews. Platform-level public totals were not refreshed for every source in the latest capture status, so this report does not present a summed public-review universe. The reviews analyzed span Oct 2017 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded.

This reading is based on more than four thousand verified public reviews captured across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google, the large majority in English. Themes and venue patterns were drawn from the full set of dated reviews, and entertainment findings reflect only the reviews that genuinely speak to atmosphere and venues. Where evidence is thin, we have said so rather than fill the gap.
