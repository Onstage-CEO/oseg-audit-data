The most distinctive fact about The Phoenician Resort and Spa is that entertainment is a genuine bright spot inside a small slice of the conversation, yet as a category it still sits below the standard this property sets everywhere else. Across a verified public review set, guests talk most about service and staff, and about the pool and amenities. Entertainment and atmosphere come up in roughly one in ten reviews. That makes it a focused, high-leverage area rather than the defining story of the property.

Within that smaller slice, the signal is mixed. When guests do describe entertainment, the average rating trails the property's own broad standard. The Phoenician draws strong, consistent marks for how it treats guests and for its pool and grounds; entertainment is the one named area that under-delivers against that bar. This is a relative gap, not a weakness in isolation, and it is the kind of gap that is reachable.

The property already owns two named entertainment moments that guests remember: the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Saturdays, and live music on weekend evenings at Thirsty Camel. Both venues carry strong standalone ratings. The opportunity is that these bright spots live in a small part of the review conversation while the wider entertainment picture rates below what guests expect of this resort.

The satisfaction landscape

Reading The Phoenician's actual theme mix, service and staff lead the conversation by a wide margin, mentioned in more than half of reviews. The pool and amenities follow closely, then food and beverage. These are the themes that define how guests experience this property, and they are largely where its four-star aggregate rating comes from. Cleanliness and room, value, and check-in and wait fill out the middle of the mix.

Entertainment and atmosphere sit at the smaller end, in roughly one in ten reviews. For these specific guests, that placement matters in a particular way: they are not choosing The Phoenician primarily for a nightly program, so entertainment rarely drives the headline of a review. But because the property performs so strongly on service and pool, entertainment stands out as the one named theme that rates below its own overall standard. In a resort where nearly everything else meets a high bar, a category that trails becomes easy to see and worth understanding closely.

Entertainment deep-dive

The headline entertainment story here is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Saturday nights. In its own reviews, the Tavern carries a strong rating, and a guest who caught the Saturday pianos described it plainly as lots of fun, alongside praise for the casual menu, the boozy milkshakes the venue is known for, and what they felt was the best wine list on the property. That is a signature moment guests remember by name, and it is doing real work for the property.

The second named venue is Thirsty Camel, which runs live music on weekend evenings. It too holds a strong standalone rating, and a guest described a mellow evening ambiance and staying until the live music ended. Beyond these two, the property's own editorial coverage points to atmosphere touches such as a live piano performance accompanying the Afternoon Tea seating, and seasonal activations like the Curse of Camelback Halloween celebration.

The pattern is that the strongest entertainment moments are concentrated in a couple of venues and specific occasions, primarily weekends. Against that, the in-venue entertainment signal across the review set rates below the property's own overall standard, and the venue mentions are spread thinly and evenly across restaurants, the pool decks and common areas, and bars and lounges, with a small number of one and two star mentions inside that group. So the watch-item is not the Tavern or Thirsty Camel on the nights they perform. It is that outside those named highlights, the entertainment experience does not yet carry the same consistency guests get from service and the pool everywhere else on the property.

The guest voice

The honest read is that guest voice on entertainment cuts both ways. Most guests who mention it speak warmly, with several five-star mentions praising the named venues and their atmosphere. At the same time, a meaningful minority were not satisfied: within a small set of entertainment mentions, two guests rated their entertainment experience one or two stars. That real count, not an inflated reading, is the case for taking this area seriously. The critical and praise quotes are shown separately below.

The opportunity

The single most valuable, reachable opportunity for The Phoenician is the gap between its two proven entertainment moments and the thinner, more inconsistent experience across the rest of the property. The dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Saturdays and live music on weekend evenings at Thirsty Camel are exactly the kind of memorable, name-recognized moments guests reward. Yet they are concentrated on specific venues and specific nights, while the broader entertainment signal across restaurants, the pool decks and common areas, and bars and lounges rates below the standard this resort meets on service and pool. The result is that guests arriving on a night or into a space without one of those signature moments encounter a quieter, less defined experience than the property's own reputation would lead them to expect, and a small but real number have said so in one and two star terms.

This matters because The Phoenician is a property that otherwise clears a very high bar. When almost every other theme, service, pool, food and beverage, meets guest expectations, the one category that trails is disproportionately visible and disproportionately fixable. The bright spots prove the property already knows how to create entertainment guests remember; the opportunity is closing the distance between those peaks and the everyday experience so the category rises to match the rest of the resort. This is a real and solvable gap. The validated, costed roadmap for closing it is exactly what the on-site diagnostic delivers.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 282 captured public guest reviews. Across sources that returned a public total, 4,241 public reviews were identified. The reviews analyzed span Jul 2025 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded. Where a source's captured count is below its public total, the difference is typically rating-only entries (a star score with no written text), which carry no analyzable content and are not part of the figures.

This report draws on 282 verified public guest reviews of The Phoenician Resort and Spa across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google, supported by deeper platform-level capture and a scan of open-web sources including travel editorial, forums, social, and Yelp. Venue-level ratings and quotes are drawn from the individual venues' own public reviews. Theme shares reflect how often each topic appears across the review set. Where the entertainment signal is small, we have said so plainly rather than overstating it, so the findings reflect what these guests actually wrote.
