The satisfaction landscape

The theme mix here is clear about what matters to these guests. Service and staff is the dominant theme by a wide margin, named in close to half of all reviews. Food and beverage comes next, then the pool and amenities, with cleanliness and room, value, and check-in and wait following behind. Entertainment and atmosphere sits at the smaller end of this list.

That ordering matters for how entertainment should be handled at The Phoenician. These are guests who notice and reward people: attentive staff, strong dining, and the pool experience. The kids activity staff draw specific praise for being high energy and upbeat, which shows guests respond to programmed energy when it is present. Entertainment is not where most guests start, but it is closely tied to the service and food and beverage moments they care about most, which is what makes it worth getting right.

Entertainment deep-dive

In-venue entertainment at The Phoenician rates about on par with the property's overall standard, and the named venues tell a consistent story. The bars and lounges draw the most entertainment mentions of any venue, and the praise there is specific: live music at the thirsty camel lounge, and a lively happy hour with music in the lobby. The restaurants are the second most mentioned venue, including the high English tea, where a guest noted the piano music as part of the ambiance. The pool decks and common areas account for the smallest group of mentions.

The pattern is that live music in the bars and lounges is the clearest strength, and the atmosphere works best when sound and energy are present in those social spaces. The watch-item is the inverse. The same lobby that earns praise for a lively happy hour also drew a comment that the food and drink in the lobby was less impressive, and a guest at the tea noted the music as the one thing that could have lifted the ambiance further. So the difference between a strong evening and a flat one at The Phoenician often comes down to whether the live element is actually on in the right space at the right time.

A meaningful number of guests rated their entertainment experience poorly, which is the honest counterweight to the praise. The signal is not that the program is weak. It is that it is inconsistent across venues and moments, with the lounges leading and the lobby and quieter dining moments more variable.

The opportunity

The opportunity at The Phoenician is to take the live music that already works in the bars and lounges and make it a dependable part of the property's atmosphere rather than a happy accident of timing. The thirsty camel lounge and the lobby happy hour are the proven formats. The first concrete step is to make the live element consistent in those spaces, so a guest arriving on an ordinary evening finds the same energy a guest found on a holiday afternoon.

The lobby is the clearest single point of leverage. It earns praise for a lively happy hour with music and a separate note that the food and drink there fell short, which means the music is doing the work of carrying that space. Strengthening and scheduling the lobby program directly supports the atmosphere guests already respond to.

One practical fix surfaces straight from the reviews: a guest asked for the activity schedule at check-in and found it was not provided, while the same guest praised the activities themselves. Publishing a clear, visible schedule of live music and programming, at check-in and in the lounges, would let more guests find the moments that already rate well. Given that service and food and beverage lead the conversation here, the right role for entertainment is to reinforce those social and dining moments, not to compete with them, and one partner managing the program across the lounges, the lobby, and the dining spaces keeps that quality even.

Methodology

The reviews analyzed span Oct 2017 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded.

This report draws on verified public guest reviews for The Phoenician Resort and Spa across Booking.com, Expedia, and Google, the majority in English. Theme shares, venue mentions, the entertainment rating, and the low-star count are computed from those reviews and rendered directly from the data. Open-web signal from travel forums and social platforms was scanned to confirm context, including discussion of Scottsdale nightlife and the resort's family and activity programming. Where the evidence is limited, the report says so plainly rather than filling the gap.
