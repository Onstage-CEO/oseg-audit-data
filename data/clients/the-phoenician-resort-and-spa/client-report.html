The defining entertainment story at The Phoenician Resort and Spa is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Friday and Saturday nights, which guests single out by name as a reason the evening lands. That signature format, alongside live music at Thirsty Camel from Thursday through Saturday, is where this property's entertainment reputation is concentrated, not spread thinly across the grounds.

Across roughly 6, 000 verified public reviews, entertainment and atmosphere appear in about 13 percent of them, making it one meaningful theme here rather than the headline. Service and staff, food and beverage, and the pool and amenities lead what guests talk about, in that order. Entertainment sits below them in volume but is closely tied to the two themes that dominate, since most of it happens inside the bars and lounges and the restaurants.

In-venue entertainment rates about on par with this property's overall standard. The aggregate sits at a strong level, and entertainment mentions track right alongside it. The useful reading is not a single verdict but where specific venues and formats pull above or below that line. The Tavern's pianos and the Thirsty Camel music pull above. The one consistent watch-item is ambient music in the pool and common areas, where a guest described being unable to find a quiet seat away from the speakers.

The satisfaction landscape

What guests choose to write about here is led by service and staff, named in nearly two-thirds of reviews, followed closely by food and beverage and by the pool and amenities. Cleanliness and rooms, value, and check-in wait times follow. Entertainment and atmosphere sits at the bottom of that ordering by volume, in about 13 percent of reviews.

That placement matters for how you read it. Because food and beverage and the bars are such heavily discussed themes here, and because the entertainment that guests notice lives almost entirely inside those venues, entertainment is not a separate program competing for attention. It rides on the same evenings guests are already rating for their drinks, their dinner, and the lounge atmosphere. When the live music or the pianos land, they reinforce the food and beverage story rather than standing apart from it.

Entertainment deep-dive

The headline is The Phoenician Tavern. Its dueling pianos on Friday and Saturday are the entertainment guests name without prompting, and the Tavern carries a solid rating across more than 250 reviews. Guests pair the pianos with the casual menu, the boozy milkshakes, and what one called the best wine list among the property's bars and restaurants. That is the signature entertainment moment here, and it is doing the work a signature should.

Thirsty Camel is the second anchor, with live music Thursday through Saturday and a comparably strong rating. Guests describe a mellow ambiance and staying through to the end of the set, which is the behavior you want: entertainment that extends the visit rather than serving as background.

Set against this property's own overall standard, in-venue entertainment rates about on par. The bars and lounges carry the largest share of entertainment mentions, the restaurants a meaningful second, and the live music inside both is consistently praised, including at high tea and at the steak house. Where the line dips is the pool and common areas, which draw the fewest entertainment mentions and also the clearest complaint. One guest across the three pools could not find a quiet seat away from amplified music, with the bathrooms described as louder still. So the pattern is specific: programmed live entertainment inside the venues works and pulls above the line, while ambient piped audio across the open grounds is the watch-item that pulls below it.

The guest voice

The honest reading is two-sided. Most guests who mention entertainment praise it, and five-star entertainment mentions far outnumber the critical ones, with the live music in the lounges and restaurants singled out repeatedly. At the same time, a meaningful minority were not satisfied: 34 guests rated their entertainment experience at the low end. That real number, not an inflated reading of it, is the case for attention, and it concentrates on ambient sound in the open areas rather than on the programmed venue sets.

The opportunity

The clearest opening is to extend what already works and fix the one place it does not. The dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern and the live music at Thirsty Camel are proven draws on the weekend nights they run; the question worth testing is whether the calendar has soft evenings, particularly midweek, where the same formats would lift the bars and lounges and the restaurants that guests already rate highly. Onstage can help line up reliable acts that match the mellow, music-stays-on quality guests describe at Thirsty Camel.

The second move is the pool and common areas. The complaint there is not about live performance but about amplified ambient audio with no quiet refuge across the three pools. A reviewed sound design, with defined quiet zones and corrected levels in the common areas and bathrooms, would remove the most concrete entertainment-linked criticism in the review set. Both moves are grounded in this property's own venues and its own guests, and one team can manage the live programming and the ambient sound plan together.

Methodology

Coverage statement: This report analyzes 6,010 captured public guest reviews. Platform-level public totals were not refreshed for every source in the latest capture status, so this report does not present a summed public-review universe. The reviews analyzed span Oct 2002 to Jun 2026. The figures below are based on the captured review set, not an assertion that every public review on every platform was downloaded. Where a source's captured count is below its public total, the difference is typically rating-only entries (a star score with no written text), which carry no analyzable content and are not part of the figures.

This report draws on roughly 6, 000 verified public guest reviews of The Phoenician Resort and Spa across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google, with TripAdvisor and Google providing the deepest coverage. Reviews were read for theme, venue, and sentiment, with entertainment mentions isolated from the wider set so that venue-level patterns could be separated from overall satisfaction. We supplemented this with a scan of open-web sources including travel forums, Yelp, and social platforms. Quotes are reproduced from public reviews and shown separately from this analysis.
