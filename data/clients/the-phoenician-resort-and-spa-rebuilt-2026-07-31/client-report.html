The entertainment findings draw on a separate, fidelity-checked layer of 6,708 all-time outlet-level OpenTable venue reviews; outlet ratings are venue-specific and are not blended into the resort-wide score.

At The Phoenician Resort and Spa, the property earns a strong, broad reputation across service, the pools, and dining, and entertainment is the one theme where guest sentiment settles below that bar. In the review set, roughly one in eight guests speak to entertainment or atmosphere, and when they do, the in-venue experience rates below the property's own overall standard. That is the defining finding here: this is a resort that guests praise widely, with entertainment as the relative gap rather than a signature strength.

The distinctive detail is where the entertainment story is strongest and where it is thinnest. The Phoenician Tavern's dueling pianos on Saturday nights is the piece of live programming guests single out by name and genuinely enjoy. The Thirsty Camel lobby lounge draws warm words for its live music on weekend evenings. Yet across the wider review set, the entertainment signal is small and mixed, and the property's leading themes, service and pool and food, carry the reputation while entertainment lags them.

For a resort operating at this level, a theme that trails the house standard is worth understanding precisely. The gap is real, it is specific to a handful of guest moments, and it is reachable. This report frames what the reviews show and where the opportunity sits.

The satisfaction landscape

The theme mix for The Phoenician reads clearly. Service and staff lead by a wide margin, mentioned in a majority of reviews, followed by the pool and amenities and then food and beverage. Cleanliness and room, value and price, and check-in and wait fill out the middle. Entertainment and atmosphere sits at the bottom of the named themes by share of reviews, spoken to by roughly one in eight guests.

That ordering matters for how entertainment should be read here. This is not a property where guests come primarily for a nightlife draw, so entertainment is one meaningful theme among several rather than the headline. But when guests do raise it, the average sentiment on the in-venue experience lands below the property's overall rating. So the theme is both smaller in volume and softer in score than the areas that define The Phoenician's reputation. For guests already paying for a resort that excels on service and setting, an entertainment experience that trails those strengths is a noticeable step down from the standard the rest of their stay sets.

Entertainment deep-dive

The headline entertainment story here is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern on Saturday nights. It is the live programming guests name directly and remember fondly, tied in the same breath to the tavern's casual menu, its boozy milkshakes, and one of the better wine lists on the property. The tavern carries a strong standalone rating, and its Friday and Saturday piano residency is confirmed and currently running. This is the property's clearest entertainment win, and it is worth recognizing as such.

The Thirsty Camel lobby lounge is the second bright spot, with live music on weekend evenings. Guests describe a mellow ambiance, attentive staff, and staying on through drinks because the room felt worth lingering in. The lounge rates well on its own, and its live music, a solo performer across weekend evenings, is confirmed and currently running. Its afternoon tea, with live piano at every seating, is also confirmed and currently running.

So the named venues that anchor entertainment are performing. The watch-item sits in the wider review set, where the two guest-facing entertainment areas, the pool decks and common areas and the bars and lounges, draw only a thin scatter of entertainment mentions, and that scatter is mixed rather than uniformly positive. Against a property whose service and pool and dining themes carry consistent praise, the entertainment theme's below-standard average is the signal to watch. The strength lives in two named rooms; the gap lives in how little of that strength registers across the broader guest experience.

The guest voice

The honest read is that guests who speak to entertainment mostly speak well of it, and the strongest praise clusters on the two named venues, the tavern's dueling pianos and the Thirsty Camel's live music. At the same time, entertainment as a theme averages below the property's overall standard, and within the entertainment mentions one guest rated their experience at the low end. That single low-star mention against a small set of praise is the plain case: the experience is good where it lands but does not yet reach every guest who is looking for it. The opportunity

The most valuable and reachable opportunity for The Phoenician is the distance between two entertainment rooms that clearly work and a wider guest experience where entertainment barely registers. The dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern and the live music at the Thirsty Camel earn real, specific praise, yet they surface in only a small share of the review set, and the entertainment theme as a whole rates below the standard the property's service, pool, and dining themes set. That is the gap: strong, well-liked live programming that too few guests encounter or associate with their stay, at a resort where the rest of the experience consistently clears a high bar.

Why it matters for these guests and these scores is concrete. Guests are already paying for and praising a top-tier stay; when entertainment trails that standard, it is the one part of the visit that steps down from what the rest of the property promises. The low-star entertainment mention and the mixed scatter across the pool decks and bars point to real guests whose evenings did not match the tavern-and-lounge experience others describe. This is a fixable gap, not a structural weakness, and the pattern in your own reviews shows exactly where it lives. What we do not do in a free read is hand over the solution. Methodology

Coverage statement: Property-level capture is deep and multi-year: 7,890 public review records of the resort were captured across Google, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Booking spanning 2002, 2026. TripAdvisor and Booking were captured in full (captured count equals the platform's available total); Google and Expedia were captured to deep-crawl depth, which does not include every rating-only entry (a star with no written text) or reviews beyond the crawl limit, so this is a deep capture, not a claim that every public review was downloaded. The resort-wide scores in this report are computed from the 299 of those reviews that fall in the current-state window (Jul 2025 to Jul 2026); older reviews outside that window are retained but deliberately not blended into the current-state figures. Entertainment findings draw on a separate, fidelity-checked outlet-level layer of 6,708 all-time OpenTable venue reviews (J&G Steakhouse 3,436, Thirsty Camel 1,685, Mowry & Cotton 1,164, The Phoenician Tavern 423); those venue-specific ratings are not blended into the resort-wide hotel score. Property-level and OpenTable outlet-level together total 14,598 captured records (property-level 7,890 + OpenTable outlet-level 6,708).

This report is built only on verified public guest reviews for The Phoenician Resort and Spa, drawn across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google over a twelve-month window, alongside a scan of open-web and social sources. Venue-level detail reflects each named venue's own guest reviews and its confirmed live programming. Programming status is verified against booking records. Where the entertainment signal is small, we report it plainly at that scale rather than overstating it, so the findings hold up against the scores you already know.
