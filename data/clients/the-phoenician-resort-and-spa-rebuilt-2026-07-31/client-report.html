The resort-wide scores above are computed from the 299 property-level reviews in the current-state window (Jul 2025 to Jul 2026), a recency window of the 7,890 property-level review records captured deep across 2002, 2026, not the whole history. The entertainment findings draw on a separate, fidelity-checked layer of 6,708 all-time outlet-level OpenTable venue reviews; outlet ratings are venue-specific and are not blended into the resort-wide score.

The most distinctive fact about The Phoenician Resort and Spa is that its strongest verified entertainment story is not a stage or a nightly show, it is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern, which sit inside the highest-rated dining venue on the property. That combination, a live act guests remember paired with a room they already rate highly, is a specific asset most Scottsdale resorts do not have in the same place.

Entertainment is one meaningful theme among several here, appearing in roughly one in eight reviews. It sits below the themes that define this property most: service and staff, the pool and amenities, and food and beverage lead the conversation. That places entertainment as a supporting note rather than the headline, which is the honest read for a resort whose reputation runs on service and grounds.

Within the current review window, only a small number of reviews carry verified in-scope entertainment signal, so treat the entertainment rating as directional rather than a property-wide measure. The firmer evidence is in the named programs and venues that are confirmed and currently running: the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern, live music at Thirsty Camel, and live solo piano at Afternoon Tea in the Thirsty Camel Lounge. That is where this report anchors.

The satisfaction landscape

Reading this property's actual theme mix, service and staff is the dominant subject by a wide margin, cited in a clear majority of reviews. The pool and amenities follow, then food and beverage, then cleanliness and room. Value and price and check-in and wait sit in the middle of the pack. Entertainment and atmosphere is the smallest of the tracked themes at roughly one in eight reviews.

For these specific guests, that ordering matters. The Phoenician's guests come for and talk most about service, the pools, and dining. Entertainment is not what pulls them through the door, but where it appears it is tied directly to two of those leading strengths: it lives inside the bars and lounges and inside the food and beverage experience, in the same venues guests already rate well. That adjacency is why the entertainment signal is worth reading closely even though it is a supporting theme rather than a leading one.

Entertainment deep-dive

The signature entertainment here is the dueling pianos at The Phoenician Tavern, which runs Saturday evenings. The Tavern is the busiest reviewed venue on the property and one of its highest rated, and the guest voice on the pianos is warm: guests describe landing there on a Saturday night for the pianos as a highlight, alongside the casual menu, the boozy milkshakes the Tavern is known for, and what one guest called perhaps the best wine list on the property. When a live act sits inside your strongest-rated room, that is the entertainment story to lead with.

Beyond the Tavern, live music at Thirsty Camel runs on weekend evenings, and the guest read there is equally positive: guests describe a mellow ambiance, stopping in for drinks and staying until the music ended. The Thirsty Camel Lounge also carries live solo piano at every Afternoon Tea seating, Thursday to Sunday in the afternoon, a confirmed and currently running daytime program that extends live entertainment well beyond the evening. Alongside these, recurring seasonal and holiday programming is confirmed and currently running across venues, and a poolside live-music offering runs seasonally around select summer holidays rather than as a standing series.

Set against this property's own standard, the picture is consistent. The verified live programs sit in venues that already rate at or near the top of the property, and the small number of in-scope entertainment reviews split between strong praise and a low-star mention. Because that sample is thin, treat the entertainment rating as directional, not as a measured property-wide gap. The pattern that holds up on the firmer evidence is that the live acts are concentrated in the bars and lounges and in the food and beverage experience, and that the pool and common areas carry live programming mainly on a seasonal basis. The watch-item is not the quality of any named act, it is how narrow and how weekend-and-afternoon-weighted the standing live footprint is relative to a property this size.

The guest voice

The honest read is two-sided. Most guests who mention entertainment praise it, and the verified praise is specific: the Saturday dueling pianos at the Tavern and the weekend live music at Thirsty Camel both draw guests who stayed longer because of the act. At the same time, of the small number of current-window reviews carrying entertainment signal, one guest rated their entertainment experience at the low end. With so few in-scope mentions, that single low-star note is directional rather than representative, and it should be read as a prompt to look closely, not as evidence of a property-wide problem. The opportunity

The most valuable and reachable opportunity for The Phoenician is the narrowness of its standing live footprint relative to the strength of the acts it already has. The evidence is concrete: the dueling pianos land on Saturday evenings at the Tavern, live music at Thirsty Camel runs weekend evenings, and live solo piano covers the Afternoon Tea seatings Thursday to Sunday. Those are genuinely well-received, and they are confirmed and currently running. But they are clustered into weekend evenings and afternoon seatings in two lounge-and-dining rooms, while the pool and common areas, one of the two leading themes on the property, carry live programming mainly on a seasonal, holiday-weighted basis. For a resort whose guests rate the pools and the grounds among the very things they came for, that is a visible gap between where guests spend their time and where the standing live entertainment actually reaches.

Why it matters to these guests and to your scores: entertainment here already sits inside your strongest venues, so the acts are working with your reputation rather than against it. The gap is one of footprint and cadence, not quality, and that is precisely the kind of gap that is fixable. The verified guest praise proves the appetite is real; the thin overall mention volume means the ceiling is not yet being tested. Naming the specific fix, what fills that footprint, where, and at what cadence, is the work of the paid on-site diagnostic, where the validated and costed roadmap is delivered.

Methodology

Coverage statement: Property-level capture is deep and multi-year: 7,890 public review records of the resort were captured across Google, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Booking spanning 2002, 2026. TripAdvisor and Booking were captured in full (captured count equals the platform's available total); Google and Expedia were captured to deep-crawl depth, which does not include every rating-only entry (a star with no written text) or reviews beyond the crawl limit, so this is a deep capture, not a claim that every public review was downloaded. The resort-wide scores in this report are computed from the 299 of those reviews that fall in the current-state window (Jul 2025 to Jul 2026); older reviews outside that window are retained but deliberately not blended into the current-state figures. Entertainment findings draw on a separate, fidelity-checked outlet-level layer of 6,708 all-time OpenTable venue reviews (J&G Steakhouse 3,436, Thirsty Camel 1,685, Mowry & Cotton 1,164, The Phoenician Tavern 423); those venue-specific ratings are not blended into the resort-wide hotel score. Property-level and OpenTable outlet-level together total 14,598 captured records (property-level 7,890 + OpenTable outlet-level 6,708).

This report is built from verified public reviews for The Phoenician Resort and Spa captured across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, and Google, scored within a defined analysis window, and supplemented by outlet-level review evidence for the property's named venues and a scan of open-web and social sources. Entertainment findings are separated from operational and food-and-beverage feedback so that only genuine live-entertainment signal informs the entertainment read. Where the in-scope entertainment sample is thin, findings are treated as directional and are anchored instead to the named programs and venues confirmed against Onstage Entertainment Group booking records.
